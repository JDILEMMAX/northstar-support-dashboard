## 2. Communication Norms
* **Primary Channel:** Official WhatsApp group for quick async updates, emergency alerts and daily coordination.
* **Technical Channel:** GitHub PR comments and Issue threads for code reviews, bug reports and technical discussions.
* **Expected Response Window:** Within **3 hours** during daytime working hours (8:00 AM to 10:00 PM EAT).
* **Availability Notices:** If a member will be offline or unavailable for more than 4 hours during active working periods, they must drop a quick heads-up in the WhatsApp group in advance.

---

## 3. Work Execution and Daily Deadlines
To meet the strict audit requirements set by Northstar's procurement team, we adhere to the following delivery standards:

* **Same-Day Board Updates:** Task statuses on the GitHub Project board must be moved the exact day the work is done. Batched end-of-week board updates are strictly forbidden as they ruin audit log accuracy.
* **Daily WhatsApp Standup:** Every member must drop a brief 3-bullet text update in the WhatsApp group by **6:00 PM EAT** detailing:
  1. What was completed today
  2. What is planned for tomorrow
  3. Any blockers or assistance needed
* **Anti-Black-Box Rule:** No board task may represent more than 4 hours of total work. Larger tasks must be split into smaller, checkable units before work begins.

---

## 4. Conflict Resolution and Escalation Path
Disagreements and roadblocks must be handled constructively and swiftly to keep the sprint on track.

1. **Step 1 (Peer Discussion):** First address the technical disagreement directly and respectfully within the WhatsApp group or GitHub PR comment section.
2. **Step 2 (Team Lead Mediation):** If an agreement is not reached within 3 hours, the Team Lead (Jesse) will make a binding final decision based on project constraints and scope.
3. **Step 3 (Immediate Escalation):** Per sprint rules, **zero visible activity for 2 consecutive days** triggers immediate escalation to PLP Academy facilitators. We do not wait until the final submission deadline to report missing contributions.

Every team member acknowledges that individual contribution, communication clarity, deadline adherence and charter compliance will be evaluated at the end of the sprint via the confidential Peer Reliability Index. Aggregate patterns will be reflected in final evaluations.
